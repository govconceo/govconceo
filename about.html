About Us

17 Years Working With Government Contractors

For 17 years, our team has worked with government contractors, most recently through our digital agency. We’ve seen firsthand what founders and executives deal with when procurement slows, requirements change, and political or economic shifts make it harder to plan for growth.

We built GovCon CEO to give those leaders a place to find practical answers and talk candidly with peers who understand the demands of running a federal contracting business.

Free to Use. Private by Design.

Every resource on GovCon CEO is free to access, with no paywalls, paid subscriptions, upsells, or sales pitches. We don’t collect, share, or sell your personal information.

Our Mission

We help government contracting leaders make better decisions, build stronger businesses, and deliver on the public missions they support.

What We Offer

  • Executive Peer Community. Roundtables, leadership forums, and networking where executives can compare notes, get candid advice, and explore partnerships and joint ventures.
  • Market and Regulatory Insights. Coverage of federal procurement trends, regulatory changes, mergers and acquisitions, and developments in AI and cybersecurity, with a focus on what they mean for contractors.
  • Growth Strategies. Practical guidance for emerging, small, and mid-tier contractors on pursuing new opportunities and building the capacity to deliver.
  • Leadership Resources. Executive playbooks, founder perspectives, and educational content built around the decisions GovCon leaders face.

Who We Serve

GovCon CEO serves founders entering federal contracting, mid-tier executives managing expansion, and established prime contractors adapting to a changing market.

We serve contractors nationwide, and most of our business is in the Greater Washington, D.C., area, known locally as the DMV. We want to help strengthen the region’s economy by giving local contractors the resources and connections to grow, form partnerships, and create jobs here.
