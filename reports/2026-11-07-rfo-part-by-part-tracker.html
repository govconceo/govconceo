RFO Part-by-Part Tracker: 53 FAR Parts, 8 Proposed Rules, 15 Parts Still Waiting

Key takeaways

  • Deviations first, rules second. Every active FAR part has a model deviation text on Acquisition.gov, issued between May 2, 2025 and Oct 28, 2025. Eleven have been updated since, most recently Jul 1, 2026 (Parts 16 and 52).
  • 8 of 12 proposed rules are out. The FAR Council says it "is issuing twelve proposed rules." Eight have been published: four on Jun 23, 2026 and four on Sep 18, 2026.
  • 15 parts have no proposed rule yet. They are Parts 11, 19, 22, 23, 25, 30, 31, 32, 34, 42, 43, 45, 46, 48 and 50. That covers small business, labor, cost principles, contract financing and administration.
  • All comment periods have closed. The Jun 23 rules closed Jul 23, 2026 and the Sep 18 rules closed Oct 19, 2026.
  • Paperwork comments remain open. PRA notices for Cases 2026-006, -010 and -011 accept comments through Nov 23, 2026.
  • No final rule yet. No final RFO rule was found in the Federal Register as of Nov 7, 2026. The model deviations, as adopted by each agency, remain the operative text.

Findings

Phase one was deviations; phase two is rulemaking

Acquisition.gov's FAR Overhaul deviation guide posts a model deviation text for each FAR part. Agencies adopt them through their own class deviations. Each published proposed rule says it implements Executive Order 14275, Restoring Common Sense to Federal Procurement.

A proposed rule does not change the codified FAR. Until a final rule takes effect, the FAR text that binds a contract is the clause set and any deviation in that contract.

Two parts would disappear

Case 2026-003 would remove two parts entirely:

  • Part 38 (Federal Supply Schedules). The preamble says Part 38 text "is duplicative" of the FSS coverage in GSAR subpart 538.2. It proposes to remove and reserve the part.
  • Part 51 (Use of Government Sources by Contractors). Its content would move to Part 8, and Part 51 would be eliminated.

GSA has issued its own related GSAR proposals. FSS ordering procedures were proposed Sep 22, 2026 (91 FR 60063, comments due Oct 22). Utility services were proposed Oct 5, 2026 (91 FR 63243, comments due Nov 4).

Fifteen parts are still waiting

No published proposed rule yet covers Parts 11, 19, 22, 23, 25, 30, 31, 32, 34, 42, 43, 45, 46, 48 or 50. These include:

  • Part 19. Small business programs. Its model deviation was updated Feb 20, 2026.
  • Parts 22 and 23. Labor laws, and environment and sustainability.
  • Parts 30 and 31. Cost Accounting Standards administration, and cost principles.
  • Part 32. Contract financing.
  • Parts 42 and 43. Contract administration and modifications.
  • Parts 45 and 46. Government property and quality assurance.

If the twelve-rule plan holds, the remaining four rules would be the vehicle for these fifteen parts. The Federal Register notices do not give case numbers or dates for the remaining four.

Part 52 moves with every rule

Each of the eight rules amends Part 52. Clause numbers, titles and dates will therefore change across several final rules, not one.

Case 2026-001 alone would consolidate seven security-prohibition clauses into one, 52.240-3, and five representations into one provision, 52.240-2. Case 2026-003 would remove the commercial omnibus provision and clause, 52.212-3 and 52.212-5.

Paperwork notices set a later deadline

The FAR Council published separate Paperwork Reduction Act notices for some cases:

  • Cases 2026-001 and -002. Notices of Jun 25, 2026 (91 FR 38438 and 38440), with comments due Aug 24, 2026.
  • Cases 2026-010, -006 and -011. Notices of Sep 23, 2026 (91 FR 60419, 60421 and 60420), with comments due Nov 23, 2026.

These notices request comment on the information-collection burden of the proposed changes.

What it means for contractors

Bid strategy

  • Read the solicitation's deviation list. Agencies adopt the model texts on their own schedules. The clauses in a given RFP control.
  • Watch the waiting parts. Rules for Parts 19, 31 and 42 would touch set-asides, allowable costs and administration, the areas most tied to pricing.

Compliance cost

  • Template churn. Every final rule will renumber or retitle Part 52 clauses. Budget for at least eight rounds of clause-matrix updates.
  • Two baselines. Until final rules land, teams must track both the codified FAR and agency deviations.

Execution

  • Comment where it is still open. PRA comments for Cases 2026-006, -010 and -011 close Nov 23, 2026.
  • Track by part, not by case. Part 52 appears in all eight cases.

Watch next

  • Remaining four proposed rules. No publication date has been announced in the official sources opened.
  • First final rules. None found as of Nov 7, 2026.
  • Agency deviations. Updates to model deviations continue. The most recent dates shown are Jul 1, 2026 (Parts 16 and 52).
  • Related coverage. See the GovConCEO report "Revolutionary FAR Overhaul Enters Formal Rulemaking, With No Final Rules Yet."
