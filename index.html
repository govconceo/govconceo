Five Forces Shaping Federal Contracting

Cyber compliance enforcement, the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul, budget timing, AI procurement rules, and supply-chain resiliency are the five forces shaping federal contracting right now. Every claim in these reports is tied to a primary official source — a government document opened for this briefing — with the source’s date. Secondary sources are not used as the basis of a fact.

Naming note. Official White House, OMB, and Department documents opened for this briefing use “Department of War” or “DOW” in addition to, or in place of, “Department of Defense.” This briefing uses “DoD/DoW.”
