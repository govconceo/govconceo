    FY2027 Is Running on a Continuing Resolution Through December 11

    Public Law 119-103 funds FY2027 at a continuing-resolution rate from October 1 through December 11, 2026. The White House requested $1.1 trillion in base discretionary authority for the Department of War plus $350 billion in mandatory reconciliation resources. The House passed the FY2027 NDAA on July 22, 216–212; that vote is not an appropriations law.
